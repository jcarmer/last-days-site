Settlement Availability & Shop Rules

Not every settlement boasts specialized craftsmen or alchemical workshops. Use the following guide to determine which shops exist and what inventory is stocked based on settlement size.

Settlement Type Population Inventory & Rarity Cap
Village < 1,000 Basic common items only; items > 20 gp rare (1d3 stock).
Town 1,000 – 5,000 Standard stock up to 100 gp; 1d4 Uncommon items.
City 5,000 – 25,000 Full inventory up to 500 gp; custom commissions available.
Metropolis 25,000+ Unlimited stock; masterwork tools, exotic imports, and rare items.

Item Slot Weight Reference (Nimble 5E)

  • 1 Slot: Weight up to 10 lbs. (Small tools, ropes, lanterns, individual compounds, bundled kits).

  • 2 Slots: Weight 11 lbs. to 40 lbs. (Heavy camping gear, small tents, portable rams, casks).

  • 3 Slots: Weight 41 lbs. to 100 lbs. (Large pavilion tents, heavy ladders, rolling mantlets).

The Blacksmith, Weapon Smith, Bowyer, Armor Smith, Gunsmith

Blacksmiths are common even in villages, but other types of smithies are less rare. Stores will tend to focus on just one type of good, but might contain a few special items of different types.
