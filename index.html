Latest Session Summary

Session 1

Members of the Peace Guard arrive in Velders, a small lumber village on the outskirts of the Wizard Kareka's Realm.

A plume of blue smoke could be seen from miles away - the standard signal for villages to request aid from the Peace Guards.

The party was met at the gates by a Frogfolk lumberjack named Orion.

He took them to meet with the merchant and de-facto mayor of the village of Velders, one Joffrey Tadwell.

Joffrey put them up in his warehouse where they bunked with the lumberjacks. The party learned that the lumberjacks have been frequently come under attack by gangs of giant rats. The assumption is that the giant rats are eating the slain lumberjacks, as they haven't managed to recover any bodies.

The party accompanied the lumberjacks on their route the next morning, through the forest the next day. It wasn't long before the lumberjacks were attacked again, but with the Peace Guards keeping watch, the lumberjacks were saved and the giant rats massacred.

When they caught up to the lumberjacks though, they found that one had taken an arrow to the knee. The party found evidence of a bipedal giant rat and have assumed that he was the attacker.

The party followed the trail of the rats to a cliff wall where the party's hunter was able to scale the cliffside and throw down a rope to the others.

There they left their horses until they could retrieve them.

Horses

Man's second best friend, the horse has been around since ancient times - carrying goods for humans, joining them on the battle field and the farm.

Horses tend to roll into balls when threated and their thick armor protects them from most predators.

The party picked up the giant rats trail. So focused were they on the tracks, that they nearly ran into some giant spiders carrying their captured prey. The spiders were quickly dispatched and a halfling named Phineas and his family were saved from the webbing sacks they'd been trapped within.

The Carpenters had been traveling to see the local healer, an herbalist named Marla Pyreborn.

Unfortunately it quickly became clear that the spiders had made a nest of Marla's cottage. The party held off the spider's attacks. Including, that of a half-woman, half-spider monstrosity.

There the party took a few minutes to rest and catch their breath, while checking the healer's cottage for anything of use before setting out again.
