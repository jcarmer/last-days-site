Firearms

All firearms possess the Misfire (on natural 1) and Loud Boom (alerts enemies up to 300 ft) properties. Reloading a standard firearm requires 2 actions (Reload (2 Actions)). Guns equipped with the Quick Reload modification require only 1 action to reload.
