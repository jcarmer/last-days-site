Player Shopping

In general, you can find or commission non-magical equipment (+ healing potions) as long as the cost is less than 400gp when shopping at any decent sized town/city. Note that healing potions restore HP but do not heal Wounds.

The selection and quantities might be limited in smaller settlements.
